Play Musical Garden

Brief Synopsis

Musical Garden is a browser-based audio instrument designed for toddlers and young children with minimal language skills. It uses familiar garden objects, simple visual matching and direct movement instead of technical music controls or written instructions.

Players choose colourful garden objects and drag them towards their matching shadows in the garden. Each object produces a different musical note, while horizontal movement changes its pitch. Visual feedback such as scaling, animated musical notes and snapping helps make the interaction easier to understand.

Scenario 3: Musical Toy for Toddlers

Prompt:

Design a way for young children with minimal language skills to explore music.

Context:

Browser based musical instruments offer users the ability to dynamically perform music and explore complex audio design. While these interactions allow for a depth of control, they can be intimidating for many adult users, let alone young children whose language skills are still developing.

Response to the Scenario

Musical Garden responds to this scenario by replacing technical music controls with familiar objects and direct interaction. Instead of using sliders, buttons or musical terminology, players explore sound by pressing and dragging colourful garden objects.

The matching shadows provide a simple visual goal without relying heavily on written instructions. Each object follows the same interaction pattern, making the experience easier to learn: press to activate sound, drag to change pitch, move towards the matching shadow, and release to complete the match.

This allows children to explore the relationship between movement, sound and visual feedback through play rather than through instructions.

Core Interactive Features

Musical Exploration Through Movement

Each garden object produces a different starting note, while horizontal dragging changes its pitch. Moving an object to the left lowers the pitch, while moving it to the right raises it.

This Spatial Input technique replaces technical music controls with direct movement, allowing children to explore sound in a simple and playful way.

Visual Feedback

Pressing an object makes it slightly larger, while animated musical notes appear as the sound continues. These responses help communicate that the object is active and producing sound without relying on written instructions.

Matching and Completion

Players drag colourful garden objects towards their matching grey shadows. When an object approaches the correct target, the shadow becomes more noticeable.

Releasing the object close to its target makes it snap into place and the shadow disappears, giving the interaction a clear goal and completion state.

Design System

Musical Garden uses a colourful and friendly 2D illustration style to create a playful environment for young children. Bright colours are used for draggable objects, while matching targets are shown in greyscale with lower opacity. This creates a clear visual difference between the object the player can interact with and its destination.

Motion is also used as part of the feedback system. Scaling communicates activation, animated musical notes show that sound is active, target changes indicate that the player is close to a correct match, and snapping communicates successful completion.

The layout is responsive across the required screen sizes so that the garden, draggable objects and matching targets remain visible and usable. Object size and spacing can also be adjusted across the experience to introduce more challenge while keeping the interaction pattern consistent.
